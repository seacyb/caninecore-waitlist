Operator-led

Designed from inside the business, not around a generic SaaS template.

CanineCore is being shaped through firsthand grooming and boarding operations, with infrastructure and security experience behind the build.

The objective is straightforward: reduce repetitive work, make the day easier to understand, protect business and customer data, and give independent pet-care operators technology that can grow with them.
