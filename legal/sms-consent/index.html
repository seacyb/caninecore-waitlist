CanineCore is the sender. These messages are sent by CanineCore for account security and authentication.

What you may receive

CanineCore messages may include one-time verification codes and security alerts that help protect your account. Message frequency varies based on your account activity.

Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

How consent is collected

During CanineCore account security setup, SMS consent is collected through an optional, unchecked checkbox. You choose whether to check it; it is not selected for you and is not required to create or use an account.

Manage messages

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. You may receive one final confirmation message after opting out.
