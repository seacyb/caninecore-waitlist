SMS Terms

Effective date: August 28, 2026

CanineCore SMS provides customer-care and service messages related to accounts, appointments, reservations, support requests, and other services requested by you or your pet-care provider.

Consent and frequency

By separately checking an SMS consent box or otherwise clearly opting in, you agree to receive CanineCore service messages. Message frequency varies based on account and service activity. Consent is not required to purchase. Message and data rates may apply.

Commands

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. After opting out, you may receive one final confirmation message. Re-enroll by replying START where available or through the original opt-in flow.

Support

For help, email founder@caninecore.app. We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or consent status with third parties for unrelated marketing.
